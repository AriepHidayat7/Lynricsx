# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Lynricsx to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique code, not people.
- Assume good faith. Ask a question before assuming a motive.
- Respect that maintainers decide scope. "Not now" or "not this way" is a normal
  answer to a feature request, not a personal rejection.
- Keep discussion on topic and free of spam or self-promotion.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insulting, demeaning or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment in any form, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information (address, email, employer, etc.)
  without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, code review — and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers via the contact
route listed on the repository profile, or through GitHub's
[report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) flow if you would rather
not contact the maintainers directly. Do not open a public issue about it.

Maintainers will review reports promptly and keep the reporter's identity
confidential. Possible responses, depending on severity:

1. a private clarification or warning,
2. removal of the offending content,
3. a temporary restriction from project spaces,
4. a permanent ban.
